Requests/sec is a shared cap across all workers, not per-worker — raising concurrency alone doesn't raise total throughput, it just means more requests in flight at once within the same rate. Raise both together to actually go faster. Timing is randomized, not fixed: most gaps are 0.5x-1.5x the nominal interval, with ~10% landing as a longer 3x-6x "reading" pause — so actual throughput runs somewhat below the number you set, same as real human browsing would. Default (1/s, 1 worker) is the stealthy end of the range; you can stop a running crawl any time below.
